How it works

From flight to field action in three steps

  1. 01

    Fly

    Drones survey your fields and capture high-resolution imagery of every row.

  2. 02

    Analyze

    Our AI models flag disease, pest pressure and stressed zones, mapped to where they are.

  3. 03

    Act

    Treat only the areas that need it, and track how your crop responds over time.

About GroveWings

Built to make modern agriculture smarter

At GroveWings, we are dedicated to transforming modern agriculture. Our precision farming solutions harness drones and AI to provide real-time data on crop health, soil conditions and pest infestations.

We're building technology that helps farmers raise productivity, sustainability and profitability, and we're doing it hand in hand with the farms that use it.
